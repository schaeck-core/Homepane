Instructions

  1. Connect the Waveshare ESP32-P4-86-Panel to your computer via USB-C.
  2. Click Connect & Flash Firmware above and select the serial port of your board (usually labelled USB JTAG/serial debug unit).
  3. Wait for the firmware to be written. The device will reboot automatically once flashing is complete.
  4. The flasher then asks for your Wi-Fi and sends it to the panel. You can skip this and choose the network on the panel instead (or use Ethernet).
  5. On the panel, enter your Home Assistant URL and confirm the login.

Troubleshooting

  • No serial port shown? — Make sure IDF USB-JTAG drivers are installed and the board is in download mode (hold BOOT, press RESET, release BOOT).
  • macOS: You may need to allow the serial device in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Linux: Add your user to the dialout group: sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER and re-login.
